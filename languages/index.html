Languages and accessibility

Use Time Card Ledger in the language that works for you.

Choose your app language

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Find Regional preferences and select App language.
  3. Choose a language, or choose System to follow a supported device-language preference.

These are the English setting names; the labels follow your current app language. If the app asks you to restart after changing text direction, follow that prompt.

Language, currency and overtime rules are separate settings. Choosing a language does not select your country's pay rules.

Screen-reader feedback

If a control is unclear or difficult to reach with VoiceOver or TalkBack, contact support. Tell us the app version, device platform, app language, screen-reader name and the steps that led to the problem. For a wording issue, include the visible label or what was spoken and your suggested wording.

Use a blank example rather than personal time or pay records. The support guide explains how to prepare redacted diagnostics.
